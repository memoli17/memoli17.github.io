🐾 Blockmancer — Privacy Policy

EnglishTürkçe

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Blockmancer (“the game”) is made by Mozi Tech Studios (“we”). This policy explains what information is handled when you play the game on iPhone or Android, and the choices you have.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and the game has no user accounts. We do not run our own servers for the game and we do not sell personal information.

2. Game progress stored on your device

Your coins, gems, cats, upgrades, settings and statistics are saved only on your device. On Android they may be included in your own Google device backup if you have backups switched on. Removing the game (or “Reset progress” in Settings) deletes this data; purchases you made can be restored from the store.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional reward videos that you choose to watch, occasional ads between quests, and a small banner during play. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device’s advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA when permitted), IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. How Google uses this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): the game shows Google’s consent form before personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • iPhone: iOS asks whether the game may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, ads are not personalised with the IDFA. You will see the same number of ads either way.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Google → Ads).
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rating “PG”).
  • A one-time “No Ads” purchase removes the banner and between-quest ads.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation of which item was bought so it can deliver it.

5. Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules a few local reminders on your device (for example when your free chest is ready). They are created on the device itself; no data is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game’s settings or in your phone’s settings.

6. Children

Blockmancer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, KVKK in Türkiye, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store personal data on our own systems, most requests are best handled through the controls above or directly with Google, Apple or your device settings. You can always write to us and we will help.

8. Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the game or reset your progress. Data handled by Google, Apple and ad partners is kept according to their own policies.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

10. Contact

Mozi Tech Studios — mozitechstudios@gmail.com

Altınlarınız, elmaslarınız, kedileriniz, geliştirmeleriniz, ayarlarınız ve istatistikleriniz yalnızca cihazınızda saklanır. Android’de yedekleme açıksa kendi Google cihaz yedeğinize dahil edilebilir. 3. Reklamlar (Google AdMob)

Oyun ücretsizdir ve Google AdMob üzerinden reklam gösterir: izlemeyi sizin seçtiğiniz ödüllü videolar, görevler arasında ara sıra çıkan reklamlar ve oyun sırasında küçük bir banner. Reklamları göstermek ve ölçmek için Google; cihazınızın reklam kimliği (izin verildiğinde Android Reklam Kimliği / Apple IDFA), IP adresi, cihaz ve uygulama bilgileri ile reklam etkileşimleri gibi verileri işleyebilir. Ayrıntılar: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites ve Google Gizlilik Politikası.

  • Onay (AEA, Birleşik Krallık, İsviçre ve gereken diğer bölgeler): oyun, kişiselleştirilmiş reklamlardan önce Google’ın onay formunu gösterir. Seçiminizi istediğiniz zaman Ayarlar → Reklam gizlilik seçenekleri bölümünden değiştirebilirsiniz.

  • Android: reklam kimliğinizi cihaz ayarlarından (Google → Reklamlar) sıfırlayabilir veya silebilirsiniz.
  • Reklamlar genel izleyiciye uygun içerikle (“PG”) sınırlandırılmıştır.
  • Tek seferlik “Reklamsız” satın alımı banner ve görev arası reklamları kaldırır.

Satın alımlar Apple (App Store) veya Google (Google Play) tarafından işlenir. Ödeme bilgilerinizi hiçbir zaman görmeyiz; oyun yalnızca hangi ürünün alındığına dair onayı alır ve ürünü teslim eder.
