Premium Office – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains how the Premium Office app (“the app”), published by mozitech studios (“we”), handles information. Short version: your documents stay on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not ask you to create an account.

1. Your documents and files

Documents, spreadsheets, presentations, PDFs, notes, drawings, scans and converted files are created, edited and stored only on your device. We never upload them and we cannot see them. Files leave your device only when you choose to share or save them somewhere yourself (for example with the system share sheet).

2. Camera

The camera is used only by the document scanner when you start a scan. Pictures are processed on your device and are not sent to us.

3. Storage access

The app asks for access to files so it can open, list, save and convert the documents you choose. This access is used only on your device.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from the IP address,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language),
  • ad interactions and diagnostic data (for example crashes of the ad SDK).

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google’s consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ads & purchases → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing and RevenueCat)

Premium is sold through Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iOS). Payments are handled by Google or Apple; we never see your card details. To check whether you have Premium and to restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (product, date, status). See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

6. Ad-blocker check and app integrity

To keep the free version working, the app checks on your device whether advertising addresses are blocked (for example by a private DNS) and whether the installed copy is the original one. These checks run locally; no results are sent to us.

7. Data we collect ourselves

None. We do not collect, sell or share personal data. Settings such as the tutorial status or the trial start date are stored only on your device.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Ads are limited to general-audience content.

9. Data deletion

Because your files stay on your device, you can delete them in the app or by uninstalling it. To ask about data held by Google or RevenueCat in connection with the app, contact us at the address below and we will help.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
