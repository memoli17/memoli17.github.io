Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clipora ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by MoziTech, handles user information. We believe in strict privacy and local-first architecture: your personal photos, videos, and audio clips never leave your device.

1. On-Device Media Processing & Zero Cloud Upload

Clipora is designed as an on-device video and photo editing application:

  • Media Files Stay on Device: All videos, images, and audio imported into Clipora remain exclusively on your local device storage. They are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud storage.
  • On-Device AI Capabilities:
    • Auto Subtitles: Powered by on-device Whisper models running locally. Audio is processed directly on your hardware; speech is never streamed to external servers.
    • Text Translation: Powered by on-device Google ML Kit models. Language translation takes place entirely offline once local language models are downloaded.
    • Background / Selfie Segmentation: Powered by on-device ML Kit vision models running locally on your device's GPU/NPU.
  • No User Account / No Server: Clipora does not require user registration, login credentials, or personal profiles. There is no central Clipora backend server collecting your personal details.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Clipora displays advertisements via Google AdMob to support free access and ongoing maintenance:

  • Data Collected by AdMob: AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID on Android, or IDFA on iOS subject to App Tracking Transparency consent), IP address, coarse location, and app performance/diagnostic metrics.
  • Consent Management: We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to provide GDPR (EU/UK) and ATT (iOS) consent dialogues. You have full control over personalized versus non-personalized ad preferences.
  • For more information on how Google manages advertising data, visit the Google Privacy & Terms.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Clipora Pro)

All financial transactions for Clipora Pro subscriptions (Monthly & Yearly) are processed directly and securely by Google Play Billing (on Android) or Apple App Store / StoreKit (on iOS):

  • Clipora never collects, stores, or sees your payment card numbers, bank account numbers, or billing addresses.
  • Subscription management, renewals, and cancellations are handled directly within your Google Play or Apple ID account settings.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Clipora does not transmit or store personal data or edited media on remote servers, we maintain no database of user records to delete. You can permanently erase all local project files, cache, and preferences at any time by clearing the app data in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Clipora is directed to general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

Son Güncelleme: Kasım 2026

1. Cihaz Üzerinde İşleme ve Sıfır Bulut Yüklemesi

Clipora, yerel öncelikli (on-device) mimariyle tasarlanmıştır:

  • Medya Dosyalarınız Cihazınızda Kalır: Clipora'ya aktardığınız hiçbir video, fotoğraf veya ses dosyası sunuculara veya harici bulut depolarına yüklenmez. Tüm kurgu, render ve kaydetme işlemleri doğrudan telefonunuzun/tabletinizin donanımında gerçekleşir.
  • Cihaz Üstü Yapay Zekâ Araçları:
    • Otomatik Altyazı: Cihaz üzerinde yerel çalışan Whisper ggml modelleriyle üretilir; konuşma sesiniz harici bir sunucuya iletilmez.
    • Çeviri: Cihaz üzerinde çalışan Google ML Kit modelleriyle gerçekleşir; çeviri işlemleri tamamen çevrimdışı yürütülür.
    • Arka Plan / Selfie Ayrıştırma: Cihaz üzerinde çalışan ML Kit görüntü işleme modelleriyle yerel yürütülür.
  • Hesap ve Sunucu Yoktur: Clipora üyelik, oturum açma veya hesap profili gerektirmez. Kullanıcıların kişisel verilerini toplayan merkezi bir sunucu bulunmamaktadır.
