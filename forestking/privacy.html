🌲 Forest King: Animal Legends — Privacy Policy

EnglishTürkçe

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Forest King: Animal Legends (“the game”) is developed and published by Mozi Tech Studios (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is processed, and your privacy rights when using the game on Android (Google Play) and iOS (Apple App Store).

1. Overview and Core Principles

We respect your privacy. The game does not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal identity details such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location. We do not host user profile databases or sell personal data.

2. Local Game Data (On-Device Storage)

Your gameplay progress, unlocked mythology heroes, levels, upgrades, in-game coins, gems, and settings are stored locally on your device using encrypted native preferences/storage. When enabled on your device, backup services (such as Google Drive Device Backup or Apple iCloud Backup) may include this application data according to your device settings. Uninstalling the game or clearing application storage removes local progress. Non-consumable in-app purchases can be restored at any time.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob & UMP)

Forest King is free to play and is supported by advertising via Google AdMob (rewarded videos for optional rewards, occasional interstitial transitions between battles, and menu banners). To serve and measure advertisements, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process device identifiers:

  • Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (Android AAID) or Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA, subject to App Tracking Transparency consent), and IP address.
  • Ad Diagnostics & Interactions: Performance metrics, crash reports related to ad rendering, and impression data.
  • Consent Flow (GDPR / UMP): In the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and other relevant regions, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form is presented before serving personalized ads. You can manage or revoke your consent anytime via Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS, the game explicitly requests permission before tracking. If you select "Ask App Not to Track", non-personalized ads are served.
  • Ad Rating: In-game ads are strictly filtered to the General Audience (“G”) rating suitable for all players.
  • Ad-Free Option: A one-time "Remove Ads" in-app purchase permanently disables banner and interstitial advertisements.

Learn more about Google's advertising practices: Google Partner Policies and Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing & Apple StoreKit)

In-app purchases (such as Gem Packs, Starter Pack, Forest Pass, and Remove Ads) are processed exclusively by Google Play or Apple App Store. We do not collect, process, or store your payment card numbers or banking information. The game client receives only cryptographically signed receipt tokens from the official store to validate and deliver your purchases.

5. Friend Duel (PeerJS Peer-to-Peer Multiplayer)

The game offers a real-time "Friend Duel" mode enabling two players to challenge each other:

  • Connections are established peer-to-peer (P2P) using WebRTC mediated by standard PeerJS signaling.
  • Matching relies solely on temporary, random 5-letter room codes entered by the players.
  • No Chat or Voice: The duel transmits only ephemeral game state synchronization data (character movement, attack inputs, round results). No audio, text, or personal messages can be sent or stored.
  • No player identifying data is transmitted, retained, or logged during or after duel sessions.

6. Children’s Privacy

Forest King: Animal Legends features stylized fantasy martial arts action with mythical animal warriors (no graphic violence, no blood, no real-world weapon depictions). The game is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe any personal data of a minor has inadvertently reached our services, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

7. Data Retention & Security

Because we do not operate centralized user account databases, we do not retain your personal records. Store purchase receipts and ad interactions are retained securely by Apple and Google in compliance with their industry-standard security practices and regulatory obligations.

8. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA, KVKK)

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California, or KVKK in Türkiye), you have rights regarding access, deletion, and restriction of personal data. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time via your device operating system settings (Android Settings → Google → Ads; iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). For inquiries, please reach out to us at our contact email.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Material updates will be reflected with a revised date at the top of this document.
